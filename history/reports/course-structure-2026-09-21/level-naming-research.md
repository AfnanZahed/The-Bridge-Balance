# Research: how well-known programs name their levels

**Date:** 21 September 2026 · **For:** `curriculum-state/canon/course-structure.md`, section 6 · **Method:** one DeepSeek V4.1 Flash call at `--effort high`, web search and page fetches only. Its trace was audited: it wrote only this file and made no other change.

**How far to trust it.** Every quote was checked by script against the text of the pages DeepSeek fetched. 31 of 39 quotes matched word for word. Eight did not, and they are marked **[quote not verified]** below. Do not cite a marked quote. A second script check looked for the structure words themselves in the fetched text: all were found (CS50 problem sets, Core / Getting started / Extension modules, Course and Unit Mastery, learning path, career path, skill level) except one. The CS50 rows say "weeks", and no "week" or "weeks of material" appears in what was fetched, so `course-structure.md` leaves that word out. Khan Academy's course pages did not load for the fetcher, so no real Khan course title is verified.

URL: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/support/faq Quote: "Learning paths are collections of training modules that are organized around specific roles".

EARNED: Yes - XP points, badges (per module), trophies (per learning path), achievements, and credentials/certifications. URL: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/support/faq Quote: "You can view the amount of awarded XP next to the title of each learning path, module, and unit"; also "Badges are achievements that you earn when you finish a module"; also "Trophies are granted when you finish a learning path".
NAMING: Says what is TAUGHT (role or technology), not who it is for. Top level (learning path) and second level (module) are topic/role named, e.g. "Azure Web Apps", "Power BI". URL: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/support/faq Quote: "organized around specific roles (like developer, architect, or system admin) or technologies".
