# Welcome

**Welcome to The Bridge Balance — a free curriculum from zero programming background to engineering autonomous AI agents of your own.** Two genuine Harvard credentials sit inside that arc, midway through it.

One discipline carries you the whole way: [*Spec-Driven Engineering*](#what-is-spec-driven-engineering), software engineering plus AI. You use an [*AI coding agent*](/glossary#agent) — AI software that writes and runs code for you, mostly unsupervised — to build. Later, you learn to build AI agents yourself.

## What is Spec-Driven Engineering?

**Software engineering, with AI on both sides of it.** You direct an AI coding agent to build faster today. Later in this book, you learn to design AI agents of your own. Neither half works without the other.

### The problem it solves

**The industry has split into two failing habits.** One refuses AI coding agents outright and falls behind. The other lets an agent write everything without checking it — a habit real enough now to have a name: [*vibe coding*](/glossary#vibe-coding), Collins Dictionary's 2025 Word of the Year. A 2025 METR study found AI tools made experienced developers 19% slower on complex tasks, even though the work felt faster to them.

**Spec-Driven Engineering is the discipline between both failures.** Decide what to build, and its limits, in writing, before the agent starts. Let the agent write the code — that part, it does faster than you. Then check what it produced before you own it as yours.

### Why the checking can't move to the machine

**A travel agent decides where you fly.** A real-estate agent decides which listings to show you. The word *agent* borrows that same sense of independent judgment. What actually runs is bounded software — its independence is a setting you control, not a trait it has.

**An AI coding agent's confidence never wavers, right or wrong.** That steadiness is the real risk, more than any single mistake it makes.

**Compare it to a courier's tracking page.** That page checks your parcel from outside — a scanner, a database, a system independent of the delivery itself. An AI coding agent's answer comes from the same process that wrote the code, with nothing independent double-checking it. Even a tracking page has a real limit: it confirms the parcel moved, never that what's inside is what you ordered.

**Spec-Driven Engineering leaves that check with you, every time.** Reading the agent's output before you trust it is the habit this book builds from the first chapter onward.

## How to read this book

This book is built to be read in order, not sampled.

### The shape of it

- **Stage** — one of five large arcs, 0 through 4, always in the same order.
- **Chapter** — one complete sitting, start to finish, inside a stage. Most of the book lives at this level.
- **Lesson or Part** — the exception, not the rule. When a topic is genuinely too large or too mixed for one sitting, it splits here instead of forcing you through an hour-long page.

### Why it's ordered this way

- Stage 0 starts at the history of computing, not at a first line of code. Chronological order first is why nothing later asks you to take an earlier idea on faith.
- Every chapter defines its own terms at first use, even ones an earlier chapter already covered — so a chapter you find through a search result still stands on its own.
- The same senior engineer and the same total beginner read every chapter. There's no separate "basics" track to fall into and never leave.
- Spec-Driven Engineering runs from Stage 0's history lesson to Stage 4's autonomous agents without being re-taught at each stage — it gets exercised at higher stakes instead.

### Reading conditions

- Self-paced. Come back months later, skip ahead, or re-read a chapter. Nothing expires, and nothing about your reading is tracked.
- Built for a phone and light data. Text leads, images stay few, and no video sits between you and the words.
