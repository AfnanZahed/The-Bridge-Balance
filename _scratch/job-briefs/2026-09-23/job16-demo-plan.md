# The intro demo: what the site needs, and how to record it in Recordly

Written 23 September 2026. For the owner. Read this with the site open in front of you.

The goal you set: a short, professional product intro that shows students the progress is real. Not a tour of every page. One promise, three stages, proof that work is really happening, and a clear next step.

---

## 2. The demo story

### 2.1 The main cut — 75 seconds, 16:9 (1920×1080)

One promise at the start, three stages in the middle, dated proof, then what comes next. Every caption is written to be read with the sound off.

| 2 | 6–11 | Slight scroll: the hero buttons and the start of "The curriculum" | One slow scroll, then a small settle | "Three stages. One path. Nothing to buy." |
| 3 | 11–21 | The three stage cards: Stage 00, Stage 01, Stage 02 | Slow hover left to right, pausing on each card | "Stage 0: what a computer is actually doing. Stage 1: real front-end, back-end and database work, with AI agents. Stage 2: agents you design yourself." |

Every change you would notice, newest first, in plain words." |

Two notes on the shoot:
- **Never click a Stage 0 chapter or a Stage 1/2 page on camera.** Hover only. The chapter text is being replaced, and the Stage 1/2 pages are empty until item 5 is done.
- **Scene 7:** search for "terminal" (a finished chapter) unless item 6 is done. After item 6, any query is safe.

| Scene | Seconds | What is on screen | Cursor | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–4 | Homepage hero | Still | "A free textbook: from zero to building AI agents." |

## 3. Recording in Recordly, step by step

### 3.1 Prepare Windows and the browser (15 minutes, once)

1. **Screen:** 1920×1080, display scaling **100%** (Settings → System → Display). At 125% or 150% the site renders soft and the text looks small in the video.
2. **Notifications off:** turn on Focus assist / Do Not Disturb; quit WhatsApp, Slack and mail; set the taskbar to auto-hide; clear the desktop.
3. **Browser:** a clean profile or a new incognito window (Recordly records the window, so a fresh window has no extensions, no other tabs, no toolbar clutter). Hide the bookmarks bar. Browser zoom **100%**.
4. **A fresh session matters:** the homepage search overlay opens itself once per browser session. A fresh incognito window is the simplest way to avoid it appearing uninvited.
5. **Theme:** the site follows your Windows theme, so **set Windows to Light** before recording. The light page is the designed reading surface, and scene 8 then shows the switch honestly.
6. **Window size:** make the browser window exactly 1920×1080 minus its title bar, so the recording is a clean 16:9 after a small crop.

### 3.2 Recordly settings

Recordly is the free, open-source screen recorder and editor by webadderall (recordly.dev). Windows 10 build 19041 or newer is required; **Windows 11 is fine**. Install it from the releases page: at the time of writing the newest is **v1.4.0, released 8 September 2026**, and its own notes say Windows users should install that build manually. If Windows SmartScreen warns you, check the download against the file's published checksums before running it.

- **Capture:** choose **Window**, not the whole display. On Windows 11 Recordly uses the native Windows capture helper, which hides the real cursor and supports window capture from v1.4.0.
- **Cursor:** leave the rendered cursor **on**, smoothing **on**, size a little above default, motion blur **on**, click bounce **on**. This is what makes the movement look filmed rather than scraped.
- **Audio:** if you narrate, microphone on and system audio off. If you use captions only, turn both off. Do not record the room.
- **Countdown:** three seconds. It gives you time to move the mouse into place and stop fidgeting.
- **Aspect ratio:** pick the 16:9 preset for the main cut.

### 3.3 The recording itself

1. Launch Recordly, select the browser window, set the options above, and start.
2. Work through the ten scenes in order, slowly. Move the cursor in straight lines to the next thing, pause for a beat, then act. A demo reads as calm when the pauses are deliberate.
3. Do three takes of the whole thing rather than ten takes of each scene. Doing it in one flow keeps the rhythm natural, and you will cut between takes anyway.
4. Stop. Recordly opens its editor on the recording.
5. Save immediately as a `.recordly` project so a crash costs you nothing.

### 3.4 Editing

1. **Trim** the start and the end hard: the first frame should already be the homepage at rest.
2. **Zoom:** turn on the automatic zoom suggestions, then **review every one**. Delete any zoom that fires on a cursor pass rather than a moment. Add manual zoom regions for the three beats that carry the story: the three stage cards, the stage table on the Welcome page, and the 22 September changelog entry. Nothing else needs a zoom.
3. **Cursor:** check the smoothing held on the long scrolls. Shorten any scrolling that took more than about three seconds.
4. **Frame:** a solid dark grey or a soft gradient background, padding around 8–10%, rounded corners, a soft shadow. This is the difference between "a screen recording" and "a product video", and it costs one setting.
5. **Captions:** use Recordly's caption track for the lines in the table above. Keep each caption under about eight words and on screen for at least a second and a half. Recordly can also export a subtitle file alongside the video if you want it later.
6. **Length:** if the main cut runs past 90 seconds, cut scene 8 (the theme switch) first, then scene 7 (the search). The three stages and the changelog are not optional.

### 3.5 Export

**For WhatsApp (the inline version, 16:9):**
- MP4, **1920×1080**, H.264, 30 fps.
- Quality preset low enough that the file lands **under about 16 MB**. A mostly-still website compresses well; if you are over, shorten the cut rather than dropping to 720p.
- **720p** is the fallback if the file will not come down.
- Keep the duration **under 90 seconds**.

**For a bigger screen (the version you keep and reuse):**
- MP4, 1920×1080, 30 fps, highest quality preset (about 8–10 Mbps). This one is for YouTube, the site, and anyone who asks for it.
- Send that one as a **document**, not as media, when you need the quality kept.

**For the status cut:**
- Vertical 1080×1920 (or 1080×1350), MP4, under 20 seconds, captions burned in, under 16 MB.

## 4. Pairing it with the message

1. **The first line does one job:** it says what this is and why it exists, in one sentence — "Here is where the book stands today, and what is coming next."
2. **The video comes straight after that line**, as ordinary media, so it plays inline with a tap. Under 16 MB and under 90 seconds keeps it painless on a phone and a student's data.
3. **The captions carry the message alone**, because many students watch with the sound off, standing in a queue.
4. **The text then says the three stages in one line**, in the same words the video shows on screen: Stage 0 Introduction to SDE, Stage 1 SDE Mastery (AI-Driven), Stage 2 SDE Mastery (AI-Native).
5. **Then one dated proof point**, not a claim: the changelog records every change with its date, newest first, and the link goes with it.
6. **Then what is next and when**, in one line: the Stage 1 chapters, then the learning assistant, which is a preview and not switched on yet.
7. **End with one ask only** — read the Welcome page, five minutes — and keep the whole message under about 150 words. The video shows; the words tell.
8. **Do not attach the same video twice.** Send the inline version; offer the full-quality file as a document only if someone asks for it.

**Searched, and could not confirm — flagged so you are not surprised**
1. Recordly's **capture resolution and frame-rate settings** for MP4. The official list documents GIF frame-rate and quality, export dimensions and aspect presets, but no capture-resolution or MP4 frame-rate control. Record at your native 1920×1080 and export at 1080p.

6. **Several unrelated products share the name Recordly** (recordly.site, recordly.vip, recordly.app, a Google Play app, and GitHub forks). The one described here is **recordly.dev**, repository **webadderallorg/Recordly**, AGPL 3.0. Do not download from the lookalikes.
